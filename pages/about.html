Why we built it

Make the most stressful part of running software feel boring.

At 3 a.m. a crash means users seeing errors, someone getting paged, a rushed fix, and a postmortem days later. Meanwhile the same mistake sits untouched in another service.

TraceZero handles that whole loop. It shields users in milliseconds, reproduces the bug, makes two agents argue over the fix, proves it on real traffic, finds the twins, and hands a person one decision.

The demo bugs are deliberately ordinary: a division with no zero check, and a string method on a value that can be null. Most real outages look exactly like this.
